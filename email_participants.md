# Email to participants (send about one week before the course)

**Subject:** Meta-analysis course: 10 minutes of preparation before Monday

Dear participants,

The practicals of the meta-analysis sessions are done in R, on your own laptop. To make sure we spend the time on meta-analysis rather than on installation problems, please do the following before Monday (about 10 minutes):

1. Install R (version 4.1 or later) from https://cran.r-project.org and RStudio Desktop from https://posit.co/download/rstudio-desktop/, if you do not have them yet.
2. Download the participant kit from the course website: https://dbeillouin.github.io/cours_Meta_analyses/
3. Unzip it somewhere you will find again (not in your Downloads folder), double-click `meta_analysis_course.Rproj`, open `00_install_and_check.R` and click **Source**. It installs the packages we use and ends with the message "All good".

If you get an error, or if your institution blocks package installation, please reply to this email with a screenshot before the course, so that we can fix it in advance.

The same website has the slides and the practical pages, and will stay online after the course. For those who want to go further, the notebook https://literaturesynthesis.github.io/notebook develops every session with the same data.

See you on Monday,

Damien Beillouin
