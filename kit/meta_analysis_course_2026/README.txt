META-ANALYSIS COURSE, FRB-CESAB 2026 — PARTICIPANT KIT

Before the course (10 minutes):
  1. Install R (>= 4.1) and RStudio if you do not have them.
  2. Unzip this folder somewhere you will find it again (not in Downloads).
  3. Double-click meta_analysis_course.Rproj (RStudio opens), open
     00_install_and_check.R and click "Source". Wait for "All good".
     If you see an error, send a screenshot to the organisers before the course.

During the course, always open meta_analysis_course.Rproj first, then the
script of the session:
  Tue 14:00  td_evidence_map.R   Mapping the evidence
  Wed 14:00  td_models.R         Fixed, random and multilevel models
  Wed 15:00  td_bias.R           Publication bias and sensitivity
  Wed 16:15  td_advanced.R       Meta-regression and missing data
Fill the blanks (___). Instructions and folded solutions are on the course
web page of each practical. formula_sheet.pdf is the effect-size handout.

Data: Jones et al. (2021) Scientific Data 8:212,
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-021-01000-y (prepared subsets).
To go further after the course: https://literaturesynthesis.github.io/notebook
